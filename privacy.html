Privacy Policy

What this website collects, why, and how to ask us to delete it. Last updated November 17, 2026.

  1. Home
  2. Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Smart KTeam ("we"), a digital marketing team based in Seattle, WA, handles information collected through smartkteam.com. If you have a question about it, email us at smart.kteam.us@gmail.com.

Information You Send Us

When you use our consultation form, we collect the details you type in: first and last name, business name, email address, the service you are interested in, and your message. We use them only to reply to you and to discuss the work you asked about.

The form is delivered to our inbox by Resend, an email delivery service. We keep your message in our email for as long as we need it to work with you, and we delete it sooner if you ask.

Visit Statistics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics, loaded through the Google tag, to understand how people use this site: which pages are visited, how visitors arrived (for example from Google search or social media), their approximate location, and the type of device and browser. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to do this.

We also record a few actions as events, such as sending the consultation form or clicking an email link, so we can see which pages help people get in touch. We do not send the content of your form to Google.

You can learn how Google uses this data in Google's explanation, and you can opt out of Google Analytics in any browser with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Hosting and Security

This site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, it processes technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver pages and protect the site. Our contact form also uses your IP address briefly to limit repeated submissions and spam.

Maps, Demos and Links to Other Sites

Our home page includes an embedded Google Map, which is provided by Google and may set its own cookies. Our demo websites and our Facebook, Instagram and Google Maps profiles are separate pages with their own policies.

What We Do Not Do

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers named above that help us run this site.

Your Choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing smart.kteam.us@gmail.com. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

This site is meant for business owners and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
